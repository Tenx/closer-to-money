# Revenue Blockers Reference

10 blocker types. For each: diagnostic signals, root cause, and specific unlock actions.

---

## B1 — Pricing Fog (定价混乱)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "我还没定价" / "看情况定"
- Website has no price, only "contact us" for a product that doesn't need sales
- Multiple packages with unclear differentiation
- Builder fears losing customers by being "too expensive"

**Root cause:** Treating price as a late-stage decision. Price is actually a signal of who your customer is.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Pick one price today. Raise it until someone says it's too expensive, then back off 20%.
2. Rule of thumb: charge 10x what you think is fair as a starting point, then test down.
3. If B2B: price = value delivered × 0.1 (capture 10% of the value you create)
4. Remove all "contact us" pricing for products < $1,000/mo. It kills conversion.

---

## B2 — Wrong Buyer (目标客户错误)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- Free users love the product but won't pay
- Sales conversations go nowhere ("we'll think about it")
- Users are individuals but product solves a business problem
- Product has many features but no one feature is critical to anyone

**Root cause:** Building for users (who get value) instead of buyers (who write checks).

**Unlock actions:**
1. Map user vs. buyer: who benefits vs. who has budget authority? Target the buyer.
2. If your users are individuals but your product saves companies money — sell to the company.
3. Fire your free users. Relaunch with a waiting list that requires credit card.
4. Interview 5 people who already pay for adjacent solutions. They have budget.

---

## B3 — No Urgency (没有紧迫感)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "Interesting, I'll check it out later"
- Long sales cycles with no momentum
- No clear event that triggers the purchase decision
- Product solves a "nice to have" not a "must fix now" problem

**Root cause:** Not attached to a forcing function. Customers buy when the cost of inaction exceeds the price.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Find the trigger event: what just happened that makes this urgent? (New regulation, team growth, failed audit, competitor launched)
2. Target buyers who just experienced the trigger event, not buyers who might someday
3. Create artificial urgency: founding member pricing expires, limited onboarding slots
4. Reframe from feature ("track X") to consequence ("stop losing Y every month")

## B4 — No Distribution (找不到客户)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "我发了朋友圈但没人感兴趣"
- Traffic is zero or all from direct (friends/family)
- No clear channel where target buyer congregates
- Product posted once, then silence

**Root cause:** Distribution strategy was "build it and they will come." They won't.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Find 3 places where your buyer actively asks for help with this problem (Reddit, LinkedIn groups, Slack communities, industry forums)
2. Be helpful there for 30 days before mentioning your product
3. Partner with one non-competing product that already has your buyers
4. Build one distribution asset that compounds: newsletter, SEO, community. Pick one.

## B5 — Solution-First, Not Problem-First (产品找问题)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "我做了个很酷的东西，但不知道谁会买"
- Built features users "might want" without confirming they'll pay
- Heavy product, zero customers
- Founder describes product by technology ("AI-powered..."), not by outcome

**Root cause:** Started with the solution. Customers buy outcomes, not technology.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Stop building. Talk to 10 potential customers this week. Ask only: "What's the hardest part of [domain]?"
2. Describe your product in ONE sentence starting with "I help [who] [do what] [without what pain]"
3. Sell the outcome before you have the product (pre-sell as consulting first, then automate)
4. If no one will pay $100 upfront for a promise of this outcome — the problem isn't painful enough

## B6 — Trust Deficit (信任不足)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "你们公司多大？" 顾客对规模有疑虑
- No case studies, no social proof, no named customers
- Prospect keeps asking for references but never converts
- Solo founder with no credibility in the target market

**Root cause:** Buyers need to believe you can deliver. Unproven teams can't win trust through pitch alone.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Get one named customer with a real quote. Do it for free if needed.
2. Show your work: write a detailed case study with real numbers ("helped [Company] save $X in Y weeks")
3. Partner with a trusted name in the space (co-sell, co-author, or just get them to tweet about you)
4. If enterprise: find a champion inside the company who can vouch for you internally

## B7 — Feature Completeness Trap (等功能再卖)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- "我再做几个功能就去找客户"
- Product has been "almost ready to launch" for 3+ months
- Builder lists features before listing customers
- Product does many things adequately, none exceptionally

**Root cause:** Fear of rejection disguised as product work. Shipping a complete product feels safer than getting rejected by real customers.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Delete your feature roadmap. Replace it with a customer acquisition plan.
2. Identify the ONE thing your product does that no alternative does. Cut everything else for now.
3. Set a hard deadline: "I will charge my first customer by [date 2 weeks from now]"
4. If you can't charge someone with what you have today, more features won't fix it

## B8 — Price Too Low (定价太低)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- Customers pay but it's not worth your time
- "Our price is very affordable" as a selling point
- Users treat the product as disposable (low engagement despite payment)
- You can't reinvest in growth because margins are too thin

**Root cause:** Competing on price signals you don't believe in your own value. Low price attracts the most demanding, least loyal customers.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Raise prices 2x this week. Lose 30% of customers, make the same revenue with half the support burden.
2. Segment: keep low price for individuals, introduce a business tier at 5–10x
3. Anchor to the value you create: "This saves you $X/month. We charge $Y/month."
4. Add a high-price tier even if it doesn't exist yet — it makes middle tier look reasonable

---

## B9 — Wrong Stage for the Model (商业模式与阶段不匹配)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- Building a marketplace but have neither buyers nor sellers yet
- Pursuing enterprise sales as a solo founder with no sales background
- Building freemium with no distribution to fill the top of funnel
- Chasing advertising revenue with fewer than 5,000 engaged readers

**Root cause:** Chose a business model optimized for scale, not for zero-to-one.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Switch to a model that works manually at small scale first (consulting > SaaS, direct sales > marketplace)
2. Do things that don't scale. Manually connect buyers and sellers. Be the product for your first 10 customers.
3. Defer the "right" model until you have proof of demand. Revenue is proof. Traction is not.
4. Enterprise requires a sales co-founder or a channel partner. If you have neither, sell SMB first.

## B10 — Conversion Gap (有流量没转化)

**Diagnostic signals:**
- Traffic or signups are fine but trial-to-paid is < 1%
- Users complete onboarding but don't return after day 3
- Free users never hit the paywall
- Checkout completion rate < 60%

**Root cause:** Activation problem, not acquisition problem. Users don't reach the "aha moment" before leaving.

**Unlock actions:**
1. Define your activation event: what action correlates with retention? Make that the first thing new users do.
2. Remove everything between signup and the activation event.
3. Onboard manually: get on a call with every new trial user for 2 weeks. Learn what confuses them.
4. For checkout issues: remove required account creation, add Apple Pay / Stripe Link, add social proof at checkout
